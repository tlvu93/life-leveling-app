# Alpha 1 Field Test

> **ARCHIVED 2026-08-15.** This test targets the superseded experiment-first
> journey. Current validation plan:
> [`../../product/dj-vj-prototype-plan.md`](../../product/dj-vj-prototype-plan.md).

- Status: Ready to recruit
- Journey: Onboarding → recommendation → Live Audiovisual Performer → first Quest → reflection → Atlas reveal

## Decision this test should make

After 5–10 participants, decide whether the personal loop is valuable enough to deepen before building community creation, moderation, accounts, or synchronization.

Continue when most participants can complete a reflected real-world attempt, understand why their Atlas changed, and express genuine interest in the revealed next step. Revise the personal loop when browsing is appealing but the Quest is not attempted, evidence feels performative, or the reveal does not feel earned.

## Participants

Recruit 5–10 exploration-ready adults who have at least two interests they do not yet combine into one practice. Include a mix of:

- people with and without music-production experience;
- people comfortable and uncomfortable with creative software;
- people with one hour and several hours available per week;
- at least two people using an Android phone as their primary device.

Do not recruit only existing audiovisual artists. The test is about discovery and approachable entry.

## Format

1. Run a 20–30 minute moderated setup session on the participant’s own device.
2. Ask them to complete onboarding and choose a Path without explaining product terms.
3. Stop after they start the Quest. Do not watch the real-world experiment unless they invite you.
4. Give them up to seven days to complete or deliberately abandon the Quest.
5. Run a 15-minute follow-up immediately after they save their reflection and see the Atlas reveal.
6. Use **Start over** between facilitated sessions on a shared test device.

The participant may attach a photo, video, file, link, or private note. Never ask them to publish evidence or hand it to the researcher.

## Moderator prompts

### Before onboarding

- “What are you hoping this might help you discover?”
- “Think aloud, but I will not explain labels unless you are fully stuck.”

### At recommendations

- “What do you think Atlas used to choose these?”
- “Which explanation feels personally true? Which part feels generic?”
- “Is there a Path you would actually test?”

### At Path detail

- “What do you think this practice is like now?”
- “What would stop you from starting?”
- “Does this feel like a reversible experiment or a commitment?”

### After the real-world attempt

- “Show me how you would record what happened. Keep the evidence private.”
- “Did the reflection help you notice anything you had not already decided?”
- “What changed on the Atlas?”
- “Why do you think that branch opened?”
- “Would you take the revealed next step? Why or why not?”

Avoid asking whether they “liked the app.” Ask what they understood, attempted, and would do next.

## Alpha success threshold

For the first 5–10 participants, treat these as directional thresholds rather than statistical proof:

- at least 70% start the Quest after inspecting the Path;
- at least 50% complete or deliberately stop after a real attempt within seven days;
- at least 80% of those who reflect can explain why their new branch appeared;
- at least half of those who reflect want to try the revealed next step;
- no participant believes attached evidence is public by default;
- no recurring blocker prevents more than two participants from starting.

The north-star event is still: **a reflected real-world attempt that changes the user’s route**.

## Triage after each round

Rank findings by their effect on the core loop:

1. Blocks starting or returning to the Quest.
2. Makes private evidence feel unsafe or burdensome.
3. Makes the recommendation or Atlas change hard to understand.
4. Weakens desire for the next real-world step.
5. Cosmetic or terminology preference without behavioral impact.

Fix only the top one or two repeated problems between rounds. Run the same journey again before expanding scope.

## Gate for community work

If the success threshold is met, add one curated Live Audiovisual Performer Guide and collect Attempted, Helpful, and Accurate feedback. Keep open Guide creation, voting, moderation, accounts, and cloud synchronization out of scope until that smaller trust loop proves useful.
