# Path and Guide Governance

- **Status:** Canonical product rules
- **Last updated:** 2026-08-15

Governance exists to keep an open roadmap ecosystem useful without turning one
popular author's preferences into universal truth.

## Canonical Path

A canonical Path is the stable shared destination in the Universe. Life Leveling
or an approved editorial group maintains its identity, scope, aliases, core
description, and shared Node relationships.

A canonical Path:

- describes the practice neutrally;
- explains common variants and real-world contexts;
- links to reusable Nodes and related Paths;
- hosts multiple Guides;
- does not prescribe one correct route;
- uses stable identifiers so personal Journeys and external links survive
  editorial changes.

Creating or materially changing a canonical Path requires review. Community
votes may inform the decision but do not mutate the shared Universe automatically.

## Official Guide

An official Guide is an editorially maintained starting route. It can solve
the cold-start problem and demonstrate expected quality, but it is clearly
labelled as one route rather than the canonical curriculum.

Official status does not provide paid placement or immunity from accuracy and
freshness review.

## Community Guide

A community Guide is a Creator's route through a canonical Path. It may
disagree with the official Guide when its audience, outcome, or philosophy is
different.

The Creator owns the Guide's wording and route composition. Shared Nodes remain
part of the Universe. A Guide cannot silently redefine a shared Node for every
other route.

## Personal Journey

A personal Journey is user-owned and private by default. It may be copied from,
combined from, or independent of public Guides.

A Creator or moderator cannot alter someone's Journey. When a source Guide
changes, the Explorer receives an optional comparison and chooses whether to
apply any update.

## Galaxy

A Galaxy is a curated view across Paths, Guides, and Nodes. It may be maintained
by a Creator, school, organization, or editorial team. Its curator controls the
selection and presentation, not the underlying meaning of shared Nodes.

Public Galaxies follow the same disclosure, safety, review, and versioning
rules as Guides.

## Visibility and review lifecycle

1. **Private draft:** visible only to the Creator and invited collaborators.
2. **Unlisted:** accessible through a deliberate link and imported as a
   personal copy when appropriate.
3. **Proposed:** submitted for public discovery with required metadata.
4. **Community tested:** enough credible attempt feedback exists to summarize
   outcomes.
5. **Editorially verified:** structure, claims, safety, duplication,
   disclosures, and currentness have been reviewed.
6. **Needs review:** important assumptions may be stale or disputed.
7. **Archived:** retained for provenance but removed from default discovery.

An unlisted Guide is not verified merely because somebody shared its link.

## Required public metadata

A publicly discoverable Guide includes:

- intended audience and starting point;
- observable outcome;
- required, recommended, optional, alternative, and checkpoint roles;
- estimated time and expected rhythm;
- cost, equipment, location, and access assumptions;
- safety and age considerations where relevant;
- explicit fit and non-fit context;
- author experience and relevant disclosures;
- version, last reviewed date, and change history;
- known dependencies and external resources;
- a way to report inaccurate, unsafe, duplicated, or outdated information.

## Trust and feedback

Feedback states remain distinct:

- **Saved:** I may use this Guide.
- **Adopted:** I copied or remixed it into a Journey.
- **Attempted:** I tried at least one meaningful Step.
- **Reached outcome:** I reached the Guide's stated result.
- **Helpful:** It supported my route.
- **Accurate:** Its practical claims matched my experience.
- **Needs update:** A material assumption is stale or misleading.

Public ranking should weight:

- fit to the current Explorer's stated context;
- credible attempts rather than raw views or saves;
- accuracy and helpfulness among attemptors;
- recency and response to needs-update reports;
- completeness of assumptions and disclosures;
- safety and moderation state.

Creators cannot pay to improve ranking. Concise or visually polished Guides do
not outrank accurate routes solely because they attract more clicks.

## Node proposals and duplicates

A Guide can contain provisional custom Nodes while private or unlisted. Public
review checks whether each proposal:

- maps to an existing Node;
- is a useful narrower or broader concept;
- represents a genuinely new concept;
- is primarily a resource or Guide-specific instruction rather than an Universe
  Node;
- duplicates another proposal;
- carries unsafe or unsupported claims.

Merging Nodes preserves Guide provenance and redirects stable references.

## Safety, minors, and sensitive Paths

Paths involving physical risk, health, money, minors, controlled equipment, or
regulated professions require additional review and visible scope limits.

Child-directed discovery is not enabled by relabelling an adult Guide. It
requires age-appropriate content, consent, privacy, evidence-sharing controls,
and clear separation between a child's agency and a parent's account.

## Moderation boundaries

Public discovery excludes:

- harassment, hate, exploitation, or sexual content involving minors;
- dangerous instructions without appropriate context and safeguards;
- fraudulent credentials or guaranteed outcome claims;
- undisclosed paid placement or affiliate influence;
- copied routes that do not add meaningful context;
- deceptive evidence or manipulated attempt feedback.

Private content still follows platform safety and legal requirements, but a
private route is not forced through editorial quality review.
